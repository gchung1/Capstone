# (Module 5 Part A) Business Use Cases for Admin Views

•	User management where admins can add, update, or remove users to control who can use the system
•	Content management where admins can manage articles, products, or posts for website maintenance
•	Order and transaction management where admins track orders and payments for business operations
•	Analytics and reporting where admins can view summaries and reports to make informed business decisions from analytics
•	Compliance where admins can see changes made by users to audit for errors or violations


# (Module 5 Part B) Authentication

Security Considerations:
•	Strong passwords protect accounts from being hacked
•	Multi-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security
•	Sessions should expire after inactivity
•	Use HTTPS or safety purposes
•	Role based access controls limit sensitive information to authorized users
•	Login attempts and account changes logged for monitoring

Usability Considerations:	
•	Concise login messages help users understand errors and successes
•	Simple password reset process encourages users to routinely change passwords for security purposes and keep users from getting locked out frequently
•	Feedback about login attempts increases user confidence and security
•	Sessions that remember devices or users reduce the need to log in repeatedly and increases efficiency
