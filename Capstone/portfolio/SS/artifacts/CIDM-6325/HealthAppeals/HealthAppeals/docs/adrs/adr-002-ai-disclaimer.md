# ADR-002: AI Disclaimer in Generated Letters

Date: 2025-11-14
Status: Accepted

Context

- Problem/forces:
  - AI-generated letters may contain errors or omissions.
  - Users must be aware that the AI output is not legally verified.


Decision

  - Ensures the disclaimer is always included in letters.
  - Reduces risk of misuse.
  - Simple to implement in the views.py logic.

Consequences

- Positive:
  - Users are informed of AI limitations.
  - Reduces liability.
- Negative/Risks:
  - Slightly increases letter length.

Validation

- Measure:
  - Generated letters always include: "This letter was generated by AI and should be reviewed before submission."
- Rollback:
  - Could move disclaimer to website instructions only.
