# Contribution Toward the Capstone

## Why Software and Systems Is Integratable
Software and Systems connects the other curricular areas by turning data, analytics, and organizational requirements into something users can interact with. Data Management provides the information, Data Analytics provides insights, and Networking and Cybersecurity provide connectivity and protection. Software and Systems brings these components together into a usable interactive solution.

## How It Could Be Integrated
My preliminary capstone concept is a health insurance appeals letter generator. The software would allow a user to enter or upload claim and denial information and help generate a draft appeal.

Data Management could organize claim, insurance, provider, diagnosis, service, and denial information. Data Analytics could summarize the information and identify relevant patterns or information for the appeal. Software could provide the user interface and application functionality. Generative AI could assist with creating a draft appeal while keeping the user responsible for reviewing the final result. Networking and Cybersecurity would address authentication, privacy, and protection of sensitive healthcare information.

## How It Connects to My Prior Work
The project builds directly on my coursework. My healthcare background and CIDM 6375 provide the domain knowledge. CIDM 6308 provides analytics and visualization experience. CIDM 6350 and CIDM 6351 provide database, SQL, ETL, Snowflake, and data pipeline knowledge. CIDM 6325 and CIDM 5310 provide my direct software foundation through HTML, Git, Python, Streamlit, and AI.

The project would also allow me to strengthen my weakest area by applying these existing skills to a complete system rather than treating each curricular area separately.

## New Integration Statement
My coursework has shown me that the four curricular areas are interconnected. Data Management organizes information, Data Analytics creates meaning from that information, Software and Systems makes it usable, and Networking and Cybersecurity protects it.

Although Software and Systems is currently my weakest area, my knowledge of the other three areas gives me a strong foundation for improving it. I understand the business and healthcare problems I want to solve, the data involved, the analytical needs, and the security considerations. By combining that foundation with software tools and AI assisted development, I can become more effective at building and evaluating advanced software and system solutions.
