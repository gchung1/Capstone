## Why Networking and Cybersecurity Is Integratable
Networking and Cybersecurity is integratable with the other curricular areas because data and software systems must be connected, accessible, and protected. Data Management organizes information, Data Analytics creates insights, and Software and Systems provides interactive applications for users. Networking and Cybersecurity provide the connectivity and protection that allow these components to operate properly and securely.

## How It Could Be Integrated
My preliminary capstone concept is a health insurance appeals letter generator. Networking and Cybersecurity would be essential as the system would involve sensitive healthcare, personal, and insurance information.

The system could incorporate authentication, authorization, access controls, secure transmission, and protection of stored information. A basic security assessment could also be performed routinely to identify and mitigate potential vulnerabilities.

Data Management could organize claim, insurance, provider, diagnosis, service, and denial information. Data Analytics could summarize the information and identify relevant patterns or information for the appeal. Software could provide the user interface and application functionality. Generative AI could assist with creating a draft appeal while keeping the user responsible for reviewing the final result. Networking and Cybersecurity would address authentication, privacy, and protection of sensitive healthcare information.
