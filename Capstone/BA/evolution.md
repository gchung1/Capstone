# Contribution Toward the Capstone:

## Why Data Analytics Is Integratable
Data analytics is integratable with the other curricular areas because it has become the focal point and main source of information in all areas and industries. Data management provides reliable and accessible data. Software systems provide applications through which users interact with that information. Networking and cybersecurity provide the infrastructure and protections needed to operate the solution securely. Data analytics connects these areas by turning managed data into information that can support decisions and actions.

## How It Could Be Integrated
A preliminary idea for my capstone is a health insurance appeals letter generator. This is still an early concept, but it provides an example of how the four curricular areas could work together.

Data management could organize information such as insurance plan details, claims, denial reasons, medical services, dates, diagnoses, provider information, authorization information, and supporting documentation. Data analytics could organize and summarize that information, identify patterns in denial data, and identify information relevant to an appeal.

Software systems could provide an interface for entering or uploading information, reviewing the data, and generating or editing an appeal letter. Generative AI could potentially assist in transforming structured information into a readable letter while keeping the user involved in reviewing the result.

Networking and cybersecurity would be important because the system could involve sensitive healthcare and insurance information. Authentication, access controls, secure data handling, privacy, and protection of stored and transmitted information would therefore be important considerations.
