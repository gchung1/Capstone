# Contribution Toward the Capstone

## Why Data Management Is Integratable
Data management is integratable with the other curricular areas as it provides the foundation for collecting, organizing, storing, transforming, and preparing information for use. Data analytics uses that information to generate insights and support decisions. Software systems provide applications through which users interact with the information while networking and cybersecurity support connectivity, access, privacy, and protection. Data management therefore provides the structure and reliable information needed for the other areas to function effectively.

# How It Could Be Integrated
A preliminary idea for my capstone is a health insurance appeals letter generator. This is still an early concept, but it provides an example of how the four curricular areas could work together.

Data Management could organize information such as insurance plan details, claims, denial reasons, medical services, dates, diagnoses, provider information, authorization information, and supporting documentation. The information could be structured so it can be stored, retrieved, and analyzed consistently. Data analytics could summarize the information, identify relevant patterns in denial data, and help identify information that may support an appeal. Software systems could provide an interface for entering or uploading information, reviewing data, and generating or editing an appeal letter. Networking and cybersecurity would support authentication, access controls, secure data handling, privacy, and protection of sensitive healthcare and insurance information.
