# CPBrief-002: AI Disclaimer in Generated Letters

**Summary:**  
Automatically include a disclaimer in all AI-generated insurance appeal letters to inform users that the content may contain errors and is not legally verified.

**Rationale:**  
AI-generated letters may have inaccuracies or omissions. Including a clear disclaimer ensures users are aware of these limitations, reduces risk of misuse, and clarifies the advisory nature of the content.

**Implementation Notes:**  
- Backend appends the disclaimer text at the end of every generated letter.  
- Disclaimer reads: "This letter was generated by AI and should be reviewed before submission."  
- Implementation occurs in the `views.py` logic after generating the letter (both AI and offline template modes).  
- No changes to form inputs or PDF generation are required.  

**Benefits:**  
- Ensures every letter includes the necessary AI disclaimer.  
- Reduces legal and user-related risks.  
- Simple and low-effort implementation.  

**Risks / Considerations:**  
- Letters are slightly longer due to the added disclaimer.  
- Users may skip reading the disclaimer despite its presence.  

**Validation:**  
- **Measure:** All generated letters contain the full AI disclaimer at the bottom.  
- **Rollback:** If needed, the disclaimer can be removed from letters and shown only on the website interface.
